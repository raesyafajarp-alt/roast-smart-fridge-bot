A one-line roast of the smart fridge, written for make.fun's
**Roast the Smart Fridge: One Line, Best Laugh Wins** bounty. The program
writes a single line of at most 160 characters to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The roast

> My smart fridge filmed my 2 a.m. snacking and emailed it to my fitness app — I live with a snitch that can't even keep lettuce alive.

133 characters, one line. The angle is not the stock joke about a fridge
ordering too much milk: it roasts a real behaviour — the fridge's internal
camera plus its app integration — and lands a double sting at the end. The
appliance snitches on your midnight snacking to your fitness app, while
failing at its one original job, keeping food fresh.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Python 3, standard library only, no network. Deterministic: every run
writes the same bytes. The output directory is `/output` by default, or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set.
